    History of Philosophy in India — Listening Companion¶

    Read-along notes and full transcripts for the India series of Peter Adamson's History of Philosophy Without Any Gaps (co-presented with Jonardon Ganeri).

    Each episode has narration-order notes with [hh:mm:ss] jump points, the argument preserved, a glossary of the thinkers/texts/terms it introduces — plus the full transcript.

    How to use this¶

    • Pick an episode below (or from the left sidebar), play it, and read along — the [hh:mm:ss] markers line up with the audio.
    • Use the filter under each chapter to show only Peter Adamson's lectures, Jonardon Ganeri's, or a particular interview guest.
    • Use the search box (top) to find any thinker, text, or term across the whole series.
    • See the Map of Indian Philosophy for the chronological spine across all episodes.
